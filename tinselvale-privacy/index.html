Tinselvale: Santa's Elf Town Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Tinselvale: Santa's Elf Town (Android package com.tivaroidstudio.santastycoon) is an offline Christmas village-building game. It has no account or login, and it does not send your game data or personal information to Tivaroid Studio or to third parties.

Pre-release status: the game is currently in closed testing. This policy describes the current test builds. We will review it against every release, its permissions and any third-party services, and update this page before anything changes.

1. Information handled by the game

The game stores the following on your device only:

  • Your game progress: village layout, buildings, resources, quests, achievements and similar game state.
  • Game settings, such as sound and music volume, text size, graphics quality, language and whether reminders are on.

The game does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID. It contains no Tivaroid Studio account system, no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics and no behavioral tracking.

2. Network access

The current builds do not request the Android internet permission. The game works fully offline and does not transmit data over the network.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device, such as "your workshop is full", "a daily gift is waiting" or "an Advent door is ready". They are created and delivered by your device; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in Android settings at any time.

To deliver these reminders, the game may use the Android permissions for posting notifications, restoring scheduled reminders after the device restarts, and briefly keeping the device awake while a reminder is shown.

4. Advertising and purchases

The current builds contain no ads and no in-app purchases. If optional rewarded ads or purchases are added in a future version, this page will be updated before that release to describe the providers involved (such as Google AdMob or Google Play Billing), what information they process, and how to give or withdraw consent. Ads will never be required to progress in the game.

5. Data sharing, backups and retention

We do not receive, sell or share your game data. Your progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include the game's save file in your backup under Google's terms; Tivaroid Studio does not receive it.

6. Children's privacy

The game is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

We will publish any updated policy on this page and revise the date above when material changes are made.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact tivaroid.studio@gmail.com.
