▶ Get it on Google Play

Works with: MongoDB, MongoDB Atlas (mongodb+srv://), Azure Cosmos DB for MongoDB and Amazon DocumentDB.

How to connect to MongoDB from Android

  1. Tap + New → MongoDB and paste your mongodb+srv:// or mongodb:// connection string.
  2. Enter the password (stored encrypted, not in the URL) and the default database.
  3. Connect – collections appear in the navigator.
  4. Run queries such as db.orders.find({status: "paid"}).limit(20) or an aggregation pipeline.

Private and safe by design

TivaroSQL Studio connects directly from your phone to MongoDB – there is no proxy server and no account. Passwords and keys are encrypted with Android Keystore, the app has no ads, analytics or tracking, and you can lock it or a single connection with your fingerprint.
