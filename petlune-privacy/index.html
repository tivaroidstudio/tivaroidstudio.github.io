Petlune — Privacy policy

Pre-release draft — not yet approved for Google Play. This app is listed as coming soon. This document describes anticipated data categories, not verified current production behavior. It must be reviewed against the release build, its SDKs and the Google Play Data safety declaration before publication.

Developer and contact: Tivaroid Studio, tivaroid.studio@gmail.com.

1. What the app does

Petlune is being developed for pet-care planning and tracking.

2. Data potentially processed

Pet profiles, photos, medication schedules, appointments, expenses and care notes may contain personal information about owners and other people.

Whether each category stays on the device, is shared with other users, or is transmitted to a service depends on the final implementation and user-selected features. This draft does not claim that data is never collected or shared.

3. Permissions and third parties

Photo access, notification permissions, sharing, backups, synchronization and third-party SDKs must be confirmed against the release build. The final policy will name relevant service providers and purposes, including any ads, diagnostics or cloud services.

4. Storage, retention and deletion

Storage and deletion methods are being finalized. The published policy will describe the available in-app controls, retention periods or criteria, backups and any account-deletion process, if an account is offered.

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request information about personal data, correction, deletion or restriction of processing and object to certain processing. Contact tivaroid.studio@gmail.com. Identity verification may be required to protect user data.

6. Children, security and international processing

Age suitability, security measures and any cross-border transfers will be specified after implementation review. Do not rely on this pre-release draft as a statement of final data practices.

7. Updates

This page will be replaced with a verified, effective-dated policy before the application is released publicly.
