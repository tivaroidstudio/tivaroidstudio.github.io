1. Scope

This policy applies to the Hangman: Rivals Android game and its online services. The game can be played offline, but multiplayer, friends, rankings, cloud progress, invitations and asynchronous challenges require online services.

3. Sources and service providers

  • Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Friends, Leaderboards, Multiplayer Sessions/Lobby, Relay and Cloud Code.
  • Google Play Games Services: platform sign-in and game identity.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): rewarded and interstitial advertising. The SDK may process IP address, device identifiers, product interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play: distribution, integrity, installation and crash/performance services made available by the platform.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not sell personal data. We do not provide an open text chat.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we process account and gameplay data to provide the requested service and perform our agreement with you. Security, abuse prevention, service reliability and limited game analytics are based on legitimate interests. Advertising personalization and access to device identifiers are based on consent where consent is legally required. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

5. Visibility and sharing

Your display name, selected country, score, rank, online presence and match results can be visible to other players when you enable rankings or use multiplayer/social features. Anonymous mode hides your chosen display identity from normal ranking presentation. Service providers receive only the information needed to operate their services, comply with law, prevent abuse or protect users.

6. Advertising choices

The game may display rewarded ads after a clear player choice and occasional interstitial ads between games, never during an active turn. Android and Google account controls can be used to reset or delete the advertising ID and manage ad personalization. Where required, the game will present a consent or privacy-options form before requesting personalized ads.

7. Retention

Local data remains until it is deleted in the Privacy & Account screen, the app is uninstalled, or Android clears application storage. Online game data is kept while the account is active and as needed to operate rankings, matches, fraud prevention and support. After a verified deletion request, we delete or anonymize associated data without undue delay, subject to provider processing times and narrowly limited security, legal or backup retention.

8. Account and data deletion

You can initiate deletion from Settings → Privacy & Account → Request account deletion. The authenticated game creates a one-time, 48-hour verification token for its own Unity Player ID. Only its SHA-256 hash is stored in protected Cloud Save. You can also submit a request without the app on the account deletion page; reasonable alternative verification is then required. An email or public web page does not automatically delete an account. Deletion is permanent and covers the game account and associated cloud progress, friends/relationships, challenge state and identifiable leaderboard entries. Deleting local data alone does not delete the online account.

9. Security

Cloud traffic uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Authentication tokens are handled by platform and Unity SDKs and are not shown in the user interface. The game does not request passwords for Hangman: Rivals. Local Wi-Fi and Bluetooth matches are initiated by users and should be played only with trusted nearby devices and networks. No system is perfectly secure; suspected incidents can be reported to the contact address above.

10. Children

Hangman: Rivals is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data contrary to this policy, contact us so it can be investigated and removed.

11. International transfers and rights

Service providers may process information outside your country using appropriate legal safeguards. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority. Send requests to the contact address above.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will always appear on this page. Material changes will be communicated in the game or store listing where appropriate.
